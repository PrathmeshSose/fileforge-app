01 Our Core Promise ▼

FileForge is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, privacy-respecting application. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party.

Your files are processed entirely on your device — they are never uploaded to our servers.

02 Files & Documents ▼

All file operations (PDF merge, split, image conversion, compression, encryption) happen locally on your device.

  • We never upload your files to any server.
  • Temporary files created during processing are deleted immediately after completion.
  • The Ghost Vault uses AES-256 encryption stored only in your device's private sandbox.
03 Cloud History Sync (Optional) ▼

If you sign in, we use Firebase Firestore to store a lightweight conversion log containing:

  • Output file name (e.g. merged_output.pdf)
  • Tool used (e.g. "Merge PDF")
  • A timestamp of the conversion

We do NOT store actual file content, device file paths, or sensitive file metadata.

04 Account Information ▼

If you create an account, we collect your email address (for sign-in and password reset only) and your chosen display name.

In Guest Mode, an anonymous Firebase session is created on your device. No personally identifiable information is associated with it.

05 Device Permissions ▼
  • Camera — Used by the Document Scanner to photograph documents.
  • Photo Library / Media — Used to import images for PDF conversion.
  • Storage (Android) — Used to save output files to your Downloads folder.

We never access any file outside of what you explicitly select.

06 Advertising (Google AdMob) ▼

We display banner ads powered by Google AdMob to support free access to the app. These ads may use general device information governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We request non-personalised ads by default to minimise data collection.

07 Data Security ▼
  • Firebase credentials are stored in environment variables — never hardcoded.
  • All Firestore data is scoped to the authenticated user.
  • Vault PINs are stored as SHA-256 hashes — the raw PIN is never persisted.
  • Firebase connections use TLS encryption in transit.
08 Children's Privacy ▼

FileForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at nicsicon.technologies@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

09 Changes to This Policy ▼

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
